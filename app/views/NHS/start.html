Register for adult social care data

Use this service to request full access to the adult social care data platform.

We'll review your application within 28 days.

Who can use this service

This service is open to adult social care managers, local authority staff and NHS researchers.

You can use this service if you live in England.

You can also use this service for someone else.

Before you start

You will need your:

  • full name
  • email
  • organisation

By using this service you agree to our terms of use and confirm that you have read the privacy policy.

Start now

Other ways to get your NHS number

If you cannot get your NHS number online you can:

  • find it on any letter from the NHS like a prescription or appointment letter
  • call your GP surgery and ask them for your number
